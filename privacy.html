Need 4 Speed Lahore Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Need 4 Speed Lahore handles information when you play the game on Android, Windows, or the web. The game does not require account registration.

Game progress, settings, unlocked cars, upgrades, liveries, challenge progress, credits, and local scores are stored on your device unless a platform service is required for ads, purchases, or app delivery.

Information We Collect or Process

The app may process limited gameplay and device information needed to run the game, such as app version, device type, operating system, screen size, language, control preferences, local progress, purchase ownership status from Google Play, ad consent status, and crash or diagnostic signals made available by platform services.

We do not intentionally collect your name, home address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone audio, precise location, or files from your device during normal gameplay.

Local Game Data

The game stores data locally so it can provide core features, including:

  • audio, touch, tilt-steering, and gameplay settings
  • highest stage, best score, route progress, credits, garage upgrades, liveries, and selected car
  • daily and weekly challenge progress
  • purchase ownership flags after Google Play confirms a one-time product purchase or restore

Advertising

Need 4 Speed Lahore uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads and interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device information, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and consent signals to deliver, measure, and improve ads. On Android this can include the Google Advertising ID / AD_ID when it is available and permitted by the user's device settings.

Rewarded ads are optional and are shown only when you choose an in-game reward action such as revive or bonus rewards. Interstitial ads may appear between stage attempts or stage completions. Banner ads are not used in the current gameplay build.

Purchases

The game uses Google Play Billing for one-time products such as premium cars, credit packs, and ad-free upgrade. Google Play processes payment information. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. The app receives purchase status information from Google Play so it can unlock purchased content and restore eligible purchases.

How Information Is Used

  • to run the game, save progress, restore purchases, and keep settings consistent
  • to provide optional rewarded ads and limited interstitial advertising
  • to detect and fix crashes, compatibility problems, and performance issues
  • to comply with legal, platform, tax, billing, and policy requirements

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play for app distribution, purchase processing, license testing, and install services
  • Google AdMob for advertising and ad consent where applicable
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the web version and this Privacy Policy

These services process data under their own privacy policies. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Need 4 Speed Lahore is intended for a general audience but is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the app, or reset browser storage for the web version. Purchase records may remain available through Google Play so purchases can be restored.

Your Choices

  • Use Android settings to manage advertising ID and ad personalization.
  • Use in-game settings to control sound, music, and input preferences.
  • Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove local gameplay data from your device.
  • Use Google Play purchase history and restore features for purchase-related records.
